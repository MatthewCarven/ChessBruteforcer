playing alongside and session 1's `nnue data` run going the whole time). The nets are piece-square nets
(Network.FromPieceSquares from bot\eval\eval-sf1.txt, pst-net.cs): any net of a size costs the same per
operation, and these give a search tree like a real evaluation's.

(64 positions evaluated 5,000 times each, so they stay in cache as the search's one position does; the
same timing over 4,096 positions read 359 ns for sf1, which was cache misses.)
At the probe's rates (0.65 evaluations a node; ~1.5 moves made a node, from its 13.2 column updates):
net256 costs ~1.5 x 52 + 0.65 x 55 = ~114 ns a node against sf1's 0.65 x 104 = ~68: ~46 ns more on ~820
ns a node, about -5%. The bench measured -2.1%. The probe's upper bound was -9% to -18%: it updated on
unmake as well as make, one pass per square changed, and kept today's evaluation running beside the net.

explores nearly every capture sequence. An untrained or badly scaled net would look like a hang.
