=== The probe: the search's own nodes, h512-3m and h1024-3m, bench's six positions at depth 9 ===
A scratchpad copy of the engine with hooks (probe-instrumentation.patch): a counting run records every
evaluation's value; replaying them gives the identical tree (checked: same nodes, every value equal), so arms
without the forward pass ("upd") or without the updates as well ("none") search exactly the nodes the full
engine ("full") does. full - upd = the forward pass, upd - none = the accumulator updates, none = the rest.
Timed in the thread's own CPU time (QueryThreadCycleTime at the 2.496 GHz TSC), so the time a below-normal
thread spends waiting for a CPU beside the bot doesn't count. (Wall-clock time was useless here: one arm read
0.7 to 2.1 us a node from round to round.) Arms rotated search by search; 6 rounds; medians of per-round
differences.

Where the engine's own arrays land (align.cs, by reflection on the unmodified engine, three loads of each net):
the feature weights 24 bytes past a 64-byte boundary every time, so half of today's 32-byte column loads cross
a cache line; the output weights at 48; each position's accumulator stack anywhere (0-56, varying).
