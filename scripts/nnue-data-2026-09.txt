## The rule, shared

`tune positions` and `nnue data` walk games through one function (Core/Records/QuietPositions.cs): after move 8
(ply 16 on), more than 5 pieces, side to move not in check, neither the move before nor the move played a capture
or a promotion. The score is Lichess's after the move before, i.e. the position's own; a quiet position with none
is left out. Games: rated (above), from the standard start, analysed (Evals tag), both ratings given, a result.

## The record (32 bytes; NNUE.md "Training" has the table)

  occupancy u64 at 0 (a1 = bit 0); 32 pieces as 4-bit codes at 8, in square order, low half first; flags u8 at
  24 (1 black to move, 2 mate); result for white u8 at 25 (0, 1, 2); white's score i16 at 26; game number u32
  at 28. Piece codes: colour x 8 + type (white 1-6, black 9-14). Every field is on its own alignment, so the
  trainer can view a batch as int64 / int16 / int32 without copying; that is why the result sits before the
  score, not after it as NNUE.md first listed them. Castling rights, en passant and the clocks aren't stored.
